## 1 — The problem (0:00–0:20)

Welcome to 0Gaze. [smile]

AI agents are starting to spend real money — and every custody option today leaks. Keys sit on servers. Approvals pop up on phones, where any app can see them. [pause]

0Gaze fixes both: the wallet key is born inside a hardware chip and never leaves it, and every approval renders only inside the wearer's AR glasses. [nod]

## 2 — The glasses approval, live (0:20–1:05)

Here's the live flow. The agent wants to buy inference — ten cents of USDT. [pause]

The request appears in my glasses, and nowhere else. [look at camera]

Notice what happens first: it waits for my head to be still. [pause] That's deliberate — if I nod yes while reading the card, that nod scores nothing. Capture is closed until I'm calm and ready. [nod]

Armed. Now the challenge: one deliberate look to the left… [look left, hold] …approved. [smile]

Every move is matched against my own calibrated motion signature — captured once, four directions, stored locally. Ambient head noise can't approve a payment. Neither can a stranger's head. [pause]

## 3 — The hardware trust (1:05–1:35)

Approved means the Dabao chip signs — right now, an EIP-3009 authorization, on the board itself. The key never leaves. [pause]

And there it is: settled, in USDT on Whitechain Sepolia, through our own facilitator — the facilitator pays the gas, so the wearer never holds the chain's token and never sees a chain name. [point at screen]

The receipt is on the explorer, live. Anyone can curl our 402 endpoint and decode the quote themselves. [smile]

## 4 — The agent brain + privacy (1:35–2:05)

The thinking runs on 0G Private Computer inference, and every decision is journaled as an episode — timestamp, verdict, request ID, settlement hash — anchored to 0G Storage. [pause]

If 0G is unreachable, nothing is signed: the loop fails closed, and even the refusal is journaled. Wrong motion, a stranger's head, a timeout — every denial path fails closed. That's the product: an agent that can spend, and provably cannot overspend. [nod]

## 5 — Close (2:05–2:45)

So: a private AI agent, with hardware custody, paying per request over x402, settling on Whitechain, thinking on 0G — and asking a human for consent with a single deliberate look, through glasses that only that human can see. [pause]

This is 0Gaze. The key never leaves the chip. The approval never leaves your face. [smile] Thanks for watching. [wave]
