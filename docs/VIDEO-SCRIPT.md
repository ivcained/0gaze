## 1 — The problem (0:00–0:18)

Welcome to 0Gaze. [smile]

AI agents are starting to spend real money — and every custody option today leaks. Keys sit on servers. Approvals pop up on phones, where any app can see them. [pause]

0Gaze fixes both: the wallet key is born inside a hardware chip and never leaves it, and every approval renders only inside the wearer's AR glasses. [nod]

## 2 — The glasses approval, live (0:18–1:05)

Here's the live flow. The agent wants to buy inference — ten cents of USDT. [pause]

The request appears in my glasses, and nowhere else. [look at camera]

Notice what happens first: it waits for my head to be still. [pause] That's deliberate — if I nod yes while reading the card, that nod scores nothing. Capture is closed until I'm calm and ready. [nod]

Armed. Now the challenge: one deliberate look to the left… [look left, hold] …approved. [smile]

Every move is matched against my own calibrated motion signature — captured once, four directions, stored locally. Ambient head noise can't approve a payment. Neither can a stranger's head. And the glasses know they're on my face — a proximity sensor locks the challenge the moment they come off. [pause]

## 3 — The hardware trust, settled on Whitechain (1:05–1:40)

Approved means the Dabao chip signs — right now, an EIP-3009 authorization, on the board itself. The key never leaves. [pause]

And there it is: settled, in USDT on Whitechain Sepolia, through our own facilitator — the facilitator pays the gas, so the wearer never holds the chain's token and never sees a chain name. [point at screen]

The receipt is on the explorer, live. Anyone can curl our public 402 endpoint and decode the quote themselves — x402 version 2, chain 1874, official USDT. [smile]

## 4 — The brain on 0G, the human with World ID (1:40–2:15)

The thinking runs on 0G Private Computer inference — see that line in the terminal: every request carries a 0G trace ID, and every decision is journaled as an episode, anchored to 0G Storage. [point at terminal] [pause]

Spending scales with trust: under five cents, the agent pays silently. Up to twenty-five dollars, my head motion on the glasses is the gate — what you just saw. Above that, World ID steps in: a unique human, proven present, must approve. [nod]

And every path fails closed: if 0G is unreachable, if the motion is wrong, if the glasses aren't even on a head — nothing is signed, and the refusal itself is journaled. An agent that can spend, and provably cannot overspend.
